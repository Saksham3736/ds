Greetings from Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana!

We are delighted to cordially invite your esteemed institution to GNE'S APEX 2026, a premier inter-school multi-event competition organised by the Causmic Club under the aegis of the Department of Applied Sciences, GNDEC.

Inspired by the progressive vision of NEP 2020, APEX provides a holistic platform bringing together students from Classes 9th–12th for a day of learning, creativity, innovation, healthy competition, and inter-school collaboration across academic, technological, cultural, and artistic disciplines.

Special Feature: The APEX FAIR

Complementing the competitions, the campus will transform into a vibrant celebration inspired by the festive spirit of Diwali — featuring cultural performances, traditional crafts, festive delicacies, engaging games, and interactive arenas.
