INTRODUCTION
------------
We are delighted to invite your school to GNE'S APEX 2026, a prestigious inter-school multi-event competition organised by the Causmic Club under the aegis of the Department of Applied Sciences, GNDEC.

Inspired by the vision of NEP 2020, APEX brings together talented students for a day of learning, creativity, innovation, healthy competition and collaboration through a diverse range of academic, technical, cultural and artistic events.

Complementing the competitions, the APEX FAIR will transform the campus into a vibrant celebration inspired by the festive spirit of Diwali, with cultural performances, traditional crafts, festive delicacies, exciting games and interactive activities.
